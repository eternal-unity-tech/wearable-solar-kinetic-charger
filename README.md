# Wearable Solar + Kinetic Charger

**AI-2036 Off-Grid Human Living Devices**  
https://github.com/eternal-unity-tech/wearable-solar-kinetic-charger

## Mission
A flexible, body-worn energy harvesting system that combines high-efficiency flexible solar, kinetic motion harvesting, and thermoelectric body-heat recovery. Powers personal living devices without reliance on fixed infrastructure.

## Target Specs (2036)
- Continuous power: 1–8 W average depending on activity and sunlight
- Peak: 15–25 W under full sun + movement
- Form: clothing-integrated, backpack panels, wrist/ankle modules, hat/visor
- Storage: solid-state or advanced flexible battery pack with AI charge management
- AI: adaptive harvesting prioritization, load prediction, health-aware power allocation

## Core Modules
- Flexible perovskite / organic solar layer
- Piezoelectric + electromagnetic kinetic harvesters
- Thin-film thermoelectric generators
- Edge AI power router and battery manager
- Universal low-voltage output + wireless charging for companion devices
